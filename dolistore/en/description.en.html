Why Splash for Dolibarr?

🛒 All your sales in one place

Orders and invoices from your e-commerce site, your point of sale or any other connected application land directly in Dolibarr, whatever the sales channel.

📦 Accurate stock, everywhere

A stock updated in Dolibarr is updated on all your sales channels too: no more orders for out-of-stock products. Manage a global stock, or the stock of each warehouse separately.

👥 One single customer record

With the Splash Linker, the profiles of a same customer spread across your applications are identified and merged. A change made in one place is applied everywhere.

📊 Simpler financial management

Orders, invoices and payments are imported automatically, with the right VAT rates and the right bank accounts. Your financial follow-up gets simpler... and effortless.
 

⚡ The power of the Splash platform

No rigid connector and no mappings to enter by hand: Splash analyzes each of your connected applications, discovers the data it can exchange, and automatically configures the best possible synchronization for your ecosystem. Add a new store or a new tool: it finds its place, with no development. And you always keep control to fine-tune every rule.

Built for real-life use

✅ Automatic configuration: the module detects your Dolibarr setup (multi-prices, variants, multilingual, multi-currency, multi-company, extra fields) and adapts to it on its own, with no settings to make.
✅ Frictionless import: "guest" orders linked to a default customer, customers recognized by their e-mail, products identified by their reference (SKU).
✅ VAT under control: VAT codes are recognized and, failing that, found from the rate, according to your Dolibarr dictionary.
✅ Clean addresses: addresses entered on several lines are split automatically for the applications expecting them in several fields.
✅ Multilingual: product labels and descriptions synchronized in all your languages.
✅ Your rules apply: the module acts on behalf of a dedicated user and follows the Dolibarr permissions policy.

Installation in a few minutes

  1. Download the module_splash-x.y.z.zip file, without unzipping it.
  2. In Dolibarr, go to Setup > Modules/Applications, tab Deploy/install external app/module, and upload the archive.
  3. Enable the Splash module, in the Interfaces with external systems family.
  4. Enter the identifier and the encryption key of your Splash server, then your default settings: language, user, warehouse...
💡 Can't install from the interface? Simply copy the splash folder of the archive into htdocs/custom. All the steps are detailed in the documentation.
